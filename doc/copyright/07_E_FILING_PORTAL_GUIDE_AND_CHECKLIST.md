## 📬 3. Physical Dispatch Instructions (If Required by Portal)

While the Copyright Office conducts most scrutiny online, their guidelines recommend sending one physical postal docket to ensure zero processing delays:

1. **Print 2 Sets of the Filing Docket:**
   * Signed Form XIV & Acknowledgement Slip (with Diary Number).
   * Signed Statement of Particulars & Statement of Further Particulars.
   * Notarized Affidavit on Stamp Paper.
   * Source Code Extract Dossier (First 10 & Last 10 Pages) and Technical Manual.
   * Self-attested copy of Aadhaar/PAN.
   * Bharatkosh Payment Receipt.
2. Put both sets inside a secure waterproof envelope.
3. Label the envelope:

## ⏳ 4. Statutory Timeline & Approval Stages

| Stage | Duration | What Happens |
|---|---|---|
| **1. Mandatory Waiting Period** | **30 Days** | Under Section 45 of the Act, a mandatory 30-day waiting period begins from the date of Diary Number generation to allow any public objections. |
| **2. Formal Scrutiny by Examiner** | 2 to 4 weeks | An Examiner of Copyrights reviews the source code extracts, Form XIV disclosures, and affidavit. |
| **3. Discrepancy Letter (If Any)** | 30 days to reply | If any document needs clarification, a discrepancy notice is emailed. (Our prepared documents are tailored to eliminate common objections). |
| **4. Registration Approved & Certificate Issued** | Final Stage | The entry is recorded in the **Register of Copyrights**, and an official digitally signed **Registration Certificate (ROC)** with Government of India Seal is issued and downloadable from the portal. |
