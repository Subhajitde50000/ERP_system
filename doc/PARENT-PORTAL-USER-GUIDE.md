# Parent Portal — Simple User Guide

This guide is for the grown-ups: **parents, guardians and anyone else the school has linked to a
student**. It says, screen by screen and button by button, how to use what the school has given you.
Sections 10 and 11 are for the **school office** (who ever grants or removes the access) and for the
**student** (whose record it is).

You do not need to be technical. Nothing here asks you to install or configure anything.

> Want the engineering detail instead — tables, endpoints, how access is enforced? That is
> `System.md`, "ERP + LMS — Parent–Student Connected Access". This file is only about using it.

---

## 1. What the Parent Portal is

It is your own window into **one child's** school record: today's classes, attendance, leave,
timetable, exams, marks, assignments, notices and fees.

Three things it is **not**:

| It is not | Because |
|---|---|
| Your child's account | You never log in as your child, and you cannot do their work for them |
| A two-way inbox | You can *read* almost everything and *write* one thing: a leave application |
| A payment counter | Fees are shown, never collected. Paying happens at the school, the way the school asks |

One adult can be linked to several children. One child can have several linked adults. What each
adult sees is decided **per child, per adult**, by the school — so it is normal for a mother and a
father to have different menus, and normal for you to see fees for one child and not another.

## 2. What you need before you start

| You need | Where it comes from |
|---|---|
| The **activation slip** | Handed out with the admission pack, or from the office counter |
| The **activation code** on it | 12 characters, e.g. `7QK4M2XB9RTD` — valid for 14 days, usable once |
| Your **child's roll number** | Also on the slip. It is a second check, not a formality |
| An **email** you can open right now | The portal account is created with it, and password resets go there |
| A **password of at least 10 characters** | Yours, not the child's, and not used anywhere else |
| The school's **web address** or the **app** | The app is the school's usual app — you do not install a "parent app" |

---

## 3. Step 0 — Open your account (once)

### On a computer or phone browser

1. Open the link printed on the slip, or type your school's address and add `/guardian-access`:
   `https://green.shikshasync.me/guardian-access`. The page is headed **Open your parent portal**.
2. Type the code into **Activation code**. Spaces and dashes do not matter — they are removed for
   you, and letters are capitalised as you type.
3. *(Worth doing, if the family has more than one slip)* Press **Check the invitation first**. The
   screen shows whose child it is — name, class, and whether you are the primary guardian, plus the
   day the code expires — **before** you create anything.
4. Type your **child's roll number**, exactly as printed. Without this second check, a code picked up
   off a desk gives nobody access to anything.
5. Fill **Your full name**, **Email**, **Phone (optional)**, then **Choose a password** and
   **Confirm password**.
6. Press **Create account and link**. You will see **Your portal is open** — press the sign-in link
   and you are in. No waiting for the school to approve anything.

### On the mobile app

Open the app → it asks for the **institution code** first (for Green Public School that is `green`) →
the same form appears → **Create account and link**. After activation the app remembers your school,
so the next sign-in is one tap.

### If it does not work

| The screen says | It means | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| "Invitation code not found…" | Typo, or the code was already used | Retype it carefully. The letters **I, L, O and U are never used** in a code, so every `0` is a zero and every `1` is a one. Still failing? Ask the office to reissue |
| "This invitation has expired." | Codes last **14 days** | The office can issue a fresh one in a minute |
| "The roll number does not match this invitation" | Right code, wrong roll number (or the other way round) | Check both against the slip |
| "An account already uses this email…" | You already have a portal account | **Sign in** with that account, open **My details**, and put the code in the box there. You do not need a second account |
| "Too many attempts…" | The server allows 20 code lookups and 8 account creations per hour per device | Wait an hour, or ask the office |

None of these lock your child's record. A failed guess changes nothing except the counter.

## 4. Signing in afterwards

| | Where |
|---|---|
| **Web** | `https://<school>.shikshasync.me/login` → your email and password → you land on **My family** |
| **App** | Open the app → school code (e.g. `green`) → Sign in → you land on **My family** |

Forgot the password? Use **Forgot password?** on the login page — your portal account is an ordinary
school account, so the same reset link works.

If your access is closed while you are reading something on screen, the next request says
"Your access to this student is no longer active. Contact the school office." That is the school
changing the record, not a bug on your phone.

## 5. Your first five minutes

Do these six things once and you will have seen every part of the portal.

| # | Do this | You will see |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Open **My family** | A card per child — class, roll, your relation, and tiles for attendance and dues. Press **Open** on a card to start reading that child |
| 2 | Open **Today** | Whether the child is in school, today's periods, work due, the latest notice, and who to call |
| 3 | Open **Attendance** | The overall percentage, present / absent / late / excused, a **By subject** table and a month calendar. Tap a day (app) or point at it (web) for that day's periods |
| 4 | Tap your child's name | On the app a sheet opens (**Choose a child**). On the web, if you have two or more children their names are pills under the heading — tap the other one and every screen changes |
| 5 | Open **Leave → Request leave** | The one form you can send. Fill it and you can watch the office process it |
| 6 | Open **My details** | Your phone, your address, what the school shares with you, and the box for claiming another child |

Then close the app and open it again — the child you last looked at is the child it shows you. That
is deliberate (see §8): the portal never guesses which of your children you meant.

## 6. The menu, and where it lives

| Menu item | What is there |
|---|---|
| **My family** | A card per child with their attendance and dues at a glance, what you may *not* open, invitations waiting to be claimed, and paused links |
| **Today** | Was my child at school today, today's periods, what work is due, latest notices, who to contact |
| **Attendance** | Overall %, present / absent / late / excused, per-subject breakdown, a month calendar |
| **Leave** | Absence requests you or the child filed — and the one thing you can send |
| **Timetable** | The class routine with teachers and rooms; a room change appears here |
| **Exams & results** (web) / **Examinations** + **Results** (app) | Exam dates, and the mark for each once the school publishes it; published term cards with subject-by-subject scores |
| **Assignments** | What is set, what was handed in, what it scored |
| **Notices** | The circulars addressed to your child's class or the whole school |
| **Fees** | Balance, instalments and due dates, receipts, and any concession or scholarship applied |
| **My details** | Your phone and address, what the school shares with you, and a box to claim another child |

* **Web:** the menu is the column on the left.
* **App:** tap the ☰ button at the top left — the menu is the drawer that slides out, and **Sign out**
  is at its bottom.
* The child's name is in the top bar of the app; on the web the switcher is under the page heading.

Items you do **not** have are not hidden in a corner — they are **not in the menu at all**, and at the
bottom of the app's drawer you get the plain reason:

> `Fees, Examinations` is hidden because the school has not granted it for Aarav. Ask the office if
> that looks wrong.

Type the address of a screen you were not given and you get a short explanation instead of data. Both
are the school's setting, not a fault on your device.

## 7. Using it, feature by feature

Each block below is the same shape: **Open** (where it is), **Do** (what to actually do there),
**Reading it right** (how the school means the number), **If** (what a refusal or a blank means).

### My family

**Open:** `My family` — the screen you land on, headed **Hello, {your first name}**.

**Do:**

1. Read one card per child: class · **Roll** number · academic year, then a chip with your relation and
   **"· primary contact"** if the school routes alerts to you.
2. The two tiles are the summary of the whole portal: **Attendance** (with "Last marked 30 Aug ·
   Present", or "Nothing marked yet") and **Fees due** ("Balance on the account", or **"Not shared with
   you"** when the school has not granted fees for that child).
3. **Work pending**, **Next exam**, **Results** and **Notices** are the four "do I need to act?" lines.
   "Nothing new published" under Results is the normal state, not a delay.
4. Anything you were not given is named at the bottom of the card: *"Not shared with you: Fees,
   Examinations. The school sets this per student."*
5. Press **Open** to go into that child's record, or **My details** at the top right for your own.
6. If a card says **"N days of access left"**, the school set an end date. Ask for an extension before
   it runs out — access to *new* data stops that day.

**If** the screen says **"No student is linked to your account yet"**: the office has not linked you,
or has sent a code you have not used. Any unused invitations are listed under that message with the day
their code expires — put that code in the box on the same card and press **Link my child**. Closed links
sit at the bottom under **"Links the school has paused"**, with the reason.

### Today

**Open:** `Today`. Web sections: *Today's periods*, *Work due soon*, *From the school*, *Who to call*.
App tiles: **Attendance**, **Work due**, **Next exam**, **Balance due**.

**Do:**

1. Read the **Attendance** tile — it is the answer to "is my child in school today?".
   * **app:** the small line says it directly — **"Today: Present"**, **"Today: not marked yet"**, and
     the tile turns **red** when the last mark recorded is Absent;
   * **web:** the big number is the term percentage (**amber below 75%**), with **"Last mark Present ·
     30 Aug"** under it — or "142 sessions marked" while nothing has been recorded today.
   Neither is live: it is the teacher's mark, from whenever the teacher made it.
2. Check today's periods for a **room change or a cancelled period** — that is where a school-day
   surprise shows up the earliest.
3. Read *Work due soon* before the school does: it lists what has not been submitted, with the due
   date.
4. Each section has a link to its full screen (*Full timetable*, *All notices*, …). Today is the index,
   not the record.

**Reading it right:** nothing here is real-time. A teacher marks attendance per period, often near
the end of the day, so an empty tile at 10am is normal and not an absence.

### Attendance

**Open:** `Attendance` — the screen is headed "{child}'s attendance", subtitle "Marked by subject
teachers, published by the school".

**Do:**

1. Read the four tiles: **Overall**, **Present**, **Absent**, and **Late / excused** on the web (the
   app shows **Late** with "N excused" underneath it).
2. Move the month with **Previous month** / **Next month** — the app will not let you step past the
   month we are in, because there is nothing to mark yet.
3. Open one day:
   * **app:** tap the date and that day's lines unfold — period, subject, mark;
   * **web:** the day cell holds a coloured dot per period; point at the cell and it prints the same
     lines as text (subject, period, mark). The dots mean nothing until you read the legend under the
     calendar — Present, Absent, Late, Excused.
4. Read the **By subject** table to see *where* the absences are. Two absences every Friday morning
   look like nothing in the overall percentage and a lot in the pattern.
5. If a day looks wrong, note the **date and period** before calling anyone. The class teacher can see
   the same line you are seeing; "he was absent last month" cannot be checked.

**Reading it right:** attendance is per **period**, not per day. "Absent one period" and "absent all
day" are different records, and both can be true of the same date. A cancelled period that the teacher
never marked is not an absence — ask before you act on it.

**If** the whole month is empty: the school marks it, not you, and a holiday or an unstarted term both
look identical from here. Wait a day, then ask the class teacher.

### Leave — the one thing you can send

**Open:** `Leave` (the screen is "{child}'s leave" on web, "{child} — leave" on the app).

**Do:**

1. Press **Request leave** (web calls the same button **Report an absence**).
2. Fill it in:

   | Field | On the web | On the app |
   |---|---|---|
   | Start | **First day missed** | **From** |
   | End | **Last day missed** — "leave it blank for a single day" | **To** |
   | Why | **Reason** | **Reason** |
   | Proof | **Medical or other note (optional link)** | **Document link** |

   The web dates are a calendar picker; the app asks for `YYYY-MM-DD` as printed on the screen.

3. Write the reason the way you would tell the teacher: *"Fever since last night; seeing the
   paediatrician today"* is useful. *"unwell"* is not — it gives the teacher nothing to record.
4. Press **Send to the class teacher**. The request appears in the list under the form straight away,
   tagged **Pending**.

**The rules, so a refusal is not a surprise:**

* up to **30 days** per application;
* the end date cannot be before the start date;
* no second application that overlaps a pending or approved one for the same child;
* **a leave starting more than a week in the past cannot be filed here.** A late condonation is an
  office decision on paper, not a form;
* it goes to the **class teacher** for review and is never auto-approved — an attendance record is a
  legal document, not a message.

**Reading it right:** each request carries who filed it — **Filed by you** / **Filed by another
guardian** / **Filed by the student** on the web, **By you** / **By parent** chips on the app. That tag
is the answer to "did the other parent already send this?", and the teacher sees the same distinction,
so they know whom to call back.

**If** you need to stop one: the row shows a **Cancel** button (app: **Withdraw**) only while the status
is **Pending**, and only for the adult who filed it. A request your child sent themselves is theirs to
cancel, not yours. Once a teacher has decided, the answer stands — reopen it with the office rather
than sending a second copy.

### Timetable

**Open:** `Timetable`.

**Do:**

1. Read the week as a grid: Monday to Saturday, period by period, with the subject, the teacher and the
   room.
2. Check the **period times** on the day before an early pickup or a music lesson — a school reshuffles
   periods, not usually whole days.
3. Send your child to school with what this screen says, not what last month said: a room change or a
   swapped period is published here and nowhere else.
4. Note that **today's column is highlighted** when the screen opens — it answers "what is left of
   today?" without any clicking.

**Reading it right:** an empty cell is a free period, not a data gap.

**If** the screen says **"No timetable has been published for this class yet"**: the school has not
built this week's routine — there is nothing wrong with your access.

### Exams and results

**Open:** `Exams & results` on the web (one screen, two tabs); **Examinations** and **Results**
separately on the app.

**Do:**

1. On the exam list press **Upcoming**, **Completed** or **All**. Upcoming answers "when is it and what
   still has to be revised"; Completed is where a published mark appears.
2. Open a published exam to see **Score**, out of what, the **Percentage**, the **Grade**, and whether
   it passed.
3. For a term, open the **result card**: subject-by-subject marks in one place, with the same
   percentage and grade the school printed. Exam marks and the card are **two separate permissions** —
   if you can see individual papers but no card, the school has granted Examinations and not Results,
   and that is a question for the office, not a fault.
4. Press a **completed** exam to load its marks under it: **Score** ("34 / 50", with the pass mark
   beside it), **Percentage**, **Grade**, and when the paper was submitted.
5. If a mark looks wrong, note the **exam name and subject** — the office reopens that one record.

**Reading it right:** two different things publish: an individual exam's result, and the term result
card. So a mark can be visible before the card exists. **A missing card means the school has not
published it, not that the child scored nothing.**

**Rank** is inside the class, never the year, and only when the school chooses to publish it.

**If** the card says **"No attempt was recorded for this paper, so there is nothing to mark"**: that is
not a zero. If your child was absent for it, report the absence on the **Leave** screen — a medical note
may allow a re-test.

**If** you want the answered script: that screen belongs to your child, and the school decides whether
it opens before or after review — on purpose, so a half-marked script is not discussed at the dinner
table before the teacher has finished with it. **If** you want the signed PDF card: it is issued by the
office from your child's record; this portal shows the marks, never the signed document.

### Assignments

**Open:** `Assignments`.

**Do:**

1. Use the filter along the top: **Pending**, **Submitted**, **Graded**, **Everything**. "Pending" is
   the list worth acting on.
2. Read a card — nothing has to be opened. The title, then *subject · type · "set by {teacher}"*; on
   the right, **{N} marks** and **due {date}**; under them a status chip, **Pending**, **Submitted** or
   **Graded** with the score.
3. A due date followed by **"· late"** is the school's own flag — it is written in red. That and the
   count on **Today → Work due** are the two places a missing submission announces itself.
4. A pending item with a past due date is a conversation with the child, not with the portal: nothing
   here can be fixed by a parent, and no submission can be made from it.

**Reading it right:** "submitted, not marked" is the normal state for a few days. Teachers grade on
their own schedule, and the portal reports the schedule rather than smoothing it.

**If** the list is empty: **"Nothing is due — your child is up to date."** That message is the good
news, not a loading failure.

### Notices

**Open:** `Notices`.

**Do:**

1. Read the top card. The **whole body is printed there** — nothing to open, nothing to download — so
   a notice on your phone is the same text the class notice board carries.
2. Look for the two chips that mean *act now*: **Pinned** (the school kept it at the top on purpose)
   and **Urgent** or **High** on the priority.
3. The small line at the bottom tells you who wrote it, which class it was addressed to, and the day it
   **expires**. If it asks for money or a document, that part still happens at the counter — the notice
   is not a form.
4. On the web, type in **Search this board** to find an old circular by a word in it ("sports day",
   "fee notice"). The list reloads from the school's copy of the board, and the counter beside the box
   keeps you honest: **{N} unread · {M} shown**.

**Reading it right:** you see the circulars addressed to **your child's class or the whole school**.
A notice for another section never appears here, which is also why your neighbour's message board looks
busier than yours. There is no *mark as read* for you, deliberately: the read count measures whether
the **student** looked, and ticking it from a parent account would falsify a number the school relies
on.

### Fees

**Open:** `Fees` — only if the school granted it for this child (see §6 if it is missing). The
**Fees due** tile on **My family** is the same number without the detail.

The web calls the fourth tile **Waived / scholarship**, the app just **Waived**.

**Do:**

1. Read **Balance due** first; that is the only tile that matters in a hurry.
2. Check **Net payable** (what was billed for the year) and **Paid** (what is receipted) to see how the
   balance was built.
3. Look down the **Instalments** table for the **Due** date and the status — **Pending**, **Paid**,
   **Overdue** (the late fine is printed next to it) or **Waived**.
4. Match what you paid against **Receipts**: receipt number, date, mode, reference, amount. Keep the
   receipt number for any query.
5. If the school granted a concession, it appears under **Scholarships applied**.

**Reading it right:** this is the accounts department's number, exactly, including anything they have
not yet reconciled. **Waived** is money the school has already taken off, not money you must claim.

**If** you have paid and the dues still show: the payment has not been matched to a receipt yet. Take
the **receipt number** to the office; it is fixed on this same screen, usually the same day.

**If** you do not see fees for one child but do for another: that is the school's decision per adult —
often deliberately, and it can be changed on request with a reason.

### My details

**Open:** `My details`.

**Do:**

1. Update your **phone** when it changes. Alerts and absence calls go to the number on this screen.
   Saving a new number clears its verified status, so confirm it when the school asks — otherwise
   messages keep going to the old one.
2. Update your **address** in the same block.
3. Leave the **name** alone: it is printed from the admission record and quoted in the audit trail, so
   the office changes it with documents.
4. Read the per-child list: exactly which areas the school has granted you for each child. A second
   guardian of the same child may legitimately hold a different list.
5. To add another child at this school: type the new **Activation code from the school** into the box on
   this screen and press **Link my child**. It lands on the account you are already using —
   "{name} is now linked to your account."

## 8. Two or more children

The portal always shows **one child at a time**, and the name of that child is in the page heading so a
screenshot cannot be mistaken.

* **App:** tap the child's name in the top bar → **Choose a child** opens → pick one. Every screen you
  then open is about that child.
* **Web:** when you have two or more children, their names appear as pills under the heading. Click
  one. The address bar remembers it (`?child=…`), so you can bookmark "Aarav's fees" and it opens on
  Aarav tomorrow.
* A child whose access is paused or ended **cannot be picked** — on the app they are still listed in
  **Choose a child**, greyed, with the reason; on the web the pills stay on live links and the note
  "**N links paused by the school**" sits beside them, with the full cards under
  **"Links the school has paused"** on **My family**. Deliberate: you should see that the record exists
  and why it is closed, rather than finding it quietly missing.
* Beside the names on the web you may see two honest chips: **Access until {date}**, when the school set
  an end date, and **Not the primary contact** — which is why the school phoned your co-parent and not
  you. Ask the office to move the primary flag if the alerts should come to you.
* One child only? There is nothing to switch, and no picker.

Two separate questions, two separate answers:

| Situation | What to do |
|---|---|
| Second child, **same school**, new code | **My details** → paste the code → **Link my child**. One account, both children |
| Child at a **different school** | That is a different login, on that school's address. Schools do not share one guardian account, on purpose |

## 9. When something looks wrong

| What you see | Most likely reason | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| A menu item is missing | That module was not granted for this child | Ask the office; nothing else you can see changes |
| "The school has not granted guardians access to … for this student." | Same, but you reached the screen another way (a bookmark, a shared link) | Same |
| Empty list everywhere | The school has not marked anything yet, or published nothing yet | Wait a day; then ask the class teacher |
| "This student has no active enrolment for the current academic year, so there is no record to show." | The enrolment lapsed — a promotion not yet done, or a fee hold | The office fixes the enrolment; the portal opens again by itself |
| "Access ended on …" | The school set an end date and it passed | Ask for an extension before it lapses — the portal keeps reading history but shows nothing new after |
| The child's own app shows something yours does not | Some records are the student's alone (answered papers, submissions, the signed grade-card PDF) | Normal. The office can print a grade card |
| The app shows the wrong child | It remembers the last child you opened | Tap the name in the top bar and pick (web: the pills under the heading) |
| A result card exists but the PDF is nowhere here | Parents do not get the signed-document download | Ask the office; they issue it from the student record |

If you genuinely believe a number is wrong — an attendance day, a mark, a fee — the fix is always with
the class teacher or the accounts desk. Editing from a parent account is not possible by design, so
that any change carries a staff member's name.

## 10. For the school office — granting and removing access

Web: **Admin console → Guardians** (`https://<school>.shikshasync.me/admin/guardian-links`).

### Link a guardian (one form, three shapes)

1. Choose the student (search the roster by name, roll number or class).
2. Choose how the adult gets access:
   * **Send an activation code** — the link waits as *pending*; the family sets their own password.
     This is the usual one; it costs the office no passwords.
   * **Create the login now** — an account is made and a set-password email is sent, the way staff
     invites work. Use it when the guardian cannot use email links.
   * (An existing guardian account can be attached by id through the API; in practice, issue them a
     code and they claim it from **My details**, which adds the sibling to the account they already
     have.)
3. Set the relation, tick **primary** if this is the main contact, and tick the modules.
4. Send. The code appears **once**, in a dialog. Copy it, or read it aloud and write it on the slip.
   It is not shown again after that dialog — reissue instead of searching for it.

### What each module gives the family

| Module | The family can open |
|---|---|
| Attendance | Presence, the month calendar — **and filing leave for the child** |
| Timetable | The class routine |
| Examinations | Exam dates, and each mark once published |
| Assignments | The list, what was submitted, the score |
| Results | Published term cards |
| Notices | The child's notice board |
| Fees | Balance, instalments, receipts, concessions |

Two habits that avoid 90% of disputes:

* **Leave `Fees` off unless it belongs on.** It is the one number a shared phone or a sibling's
  laptop should not show. Keep it off for a court order that names one parent, and off for
  grandparents. It is a per-adult switch, so the other parent can still have it.
* **Exactly one primary per student.** Primary decides who gets attendance alerts and fee reminders.
  Ticking a second one moves the flag — the previous primary is demoted in the same save, which is
  why it does not fail with a database error.

### Suspend, or unlink?

| Action | Effect | Use it for |
|---|---|---|
| **Suspend** | The family stops seeing new data at once; their links stay listed with "Paused by the school"; you can restore | A dispute, a fee hold, a pending custody question |
| **Unlink** | Access removed for good; the history stays in the audit trail | A relationship that has ended, a wrong entry, a student who left |
| **End date** (`Access ends`) | Access stops on that day, on the school's own calendar | A fixed order, a term-limited arrangement |

Every one of those writes an audit row — who created, edited, suspended, unlinked or reissued, and
when — because "who removed this grandfather's access, and when" is the first question asked. The
**Note for the office** field on the same form is where the reason goes (custody orders, "mother has
no fee access per order dated …"). Staff-only; the family never sees it.

### The gap worth watching

The top of the board shows **students with no guardian at all**, and a warning when a child has linked
adults but **no active primary contact** (then alerts have no recipient). Those two numbers are the
board's real job — a portal nobody was given access to is not a portal.

### Codes: do and don't

* **Do** hand the slip to the person named on it, or email the code to the address recorded on the
  link, or use **Send code** on a pending row to email a fresh one (the old one stops working
  immediately).
* **Don't** paste a code into a URL, a group chat, or a shared spreadsheet. It is a key, not an ID.
* **Don't** reissue "just to check" while the family is at the counter — that kills the code they are
  holding. Look the row up and read the state instead.
* Guardian access is offered to **school** tenants. A college tenant sees an explanation, not an
  empty table; its students hold their own accounts. Existing college links stay readable and
  removable so a tenant that changed type can clean up.

## 11. For students — what your guardians can see

* They see what the school granted them: usually attendance, timetable, notices, sometimes marks and
  fees. The school decides, and can decide differently for each of your parents.
* They **cannot**: submit your assignments, open your answered papers, download your signed grade
  card, mark your notices as read, or change anything on your record.
* They **can** file a leave for you and cancel one they filed — and you will see it in your own leave
  list marked as coming from your parent, because the teacher needs to know who asked.
* Nothing your parent can see was hidden from you; usually it is the reverse.

If something on their screen looks wrong to you, tell the class teacher — the same route as everything
else.

## 12. Staying safe

* Your code is only yours, and only until it is used. Once it works, it is gone from the system.
* One account, one family. Do not share your password with a relative who wants to "just look at the
  fees" — ask the office to link them, and they can be given attendance and notices only.
* This portal never asks for a card number, a UPI PIN, or a payment. A message that does is not from
  your school.
* The portal address starts with `https://` and is your school's own address. Do not activate a code
  from a link that arrived by SMS or WhatsApp — open the school's site and type `/guardian-access`.
* Sign out on a shared device (app: the drawer at the top left → **Sign out**).

## 13. Questions people actually ask

**Can I see my child's attendance live, minute by minute?** No — the teacher marks attendance per
period, usually by the end of the day.

**Why does my co-parent have fees and I don't?** The school set it per adult, often because of a
document they hold. They can change it; you can ask.

**My child changed school. Do I lose access?** Your access follows the enrolment at that school. A new
school means a new slip and a new account there.

**Can I get a PDF of the result card?** Not from here — the signed card is issued by the office, from
your child's record.

**Do I need to renew anything?** Check **My family**: a tile like "12 days of access left" appears
when the school set an end date. Ask for an extension before it runs out. On a child's own page the
same fact appears as "Access until …".

**Is there an app?** Yes — the same shikshasync.me app, signed in with your guardian account. It has the same
screens, including leave filing.

**I entered the code wrongly a few times. Did I lock my child out?** No. Lookups are limited per
device per hour, and after an hour you can try again. The link itself is untouched until someone
successfully claims it.

**My child's class teacher changed. Will I notice?** Yes, in two places: the class teacher's name on
**Today → Who to call**, and the room/period changes on **Timetable**.
